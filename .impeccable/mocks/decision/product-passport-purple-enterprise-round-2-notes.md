# Product Passport — purple enterprise reroll

The prior Vault Transfer, Cinematic Shelf, and Lens Stack hand is rejected and does not carry forward.

## Shared design contract

- Dark-only graphite and optical black surfaces.
- Purple is a rare material accent: a glass edge, caustic reflection, or active lens. It does not encode product truth.
- Wide editorial neo-grotesk typography, monumental regular/light numerals, mixed case, restrained italic supporting labels, and tabular figures.
- No dashboard card grid, nested cards, invented chart, score, progress visualization, medical target, or hidden factual state.
- The complete product image occupies the full left hero bay.
- The lower information section remains a four-column ledger in this order: Product metadata, Observed facts, Source record, Ranking eligibility.
- Product meaning remains legible without color, image, or motion.

## Motion contract

- Product facts are visible by default and never move for decoration.
- The route may have one rare explanatory entrance only; interactive responses stay below 300 ms.
- Button press feedback: transform to 0.97 over 120–160 ms with strong ease-out.
- Tooltips: 125–180 ms, origin-aware, with subsequent tooltips instant.
- Reduced motion removes positional movement and leaves facts in their settled state.
- Keyboard-triggered actions receive no entrance animation.
